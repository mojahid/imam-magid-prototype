01 · SUDAN · EARLY LIFE

Roots along the Nile

Imam Magid describes growing up in a village along the Nile in northern Sudan. Education and scholarship were deeply valued within his family and community. His father was an Islamic scholar and educator whose work connected religious teaching with care for people in need.

First-person source · Georgetown ↗︎
02 · SCHOLARSHIP & EDUCATION

Learning with purpose

His Islamic studies in Sudan were shaped by his father and other teachers. In a 2022 interview, he recalled an early interest in law, justice and social service. He later sought to understand how religious teaching could respond meaningfully to contemporary social questions.

03 · 1987–1988 · UNITED STATES

An unexpected new chapter

Imam Magid recalls arriving in the United States around 1987 or early 1988 during his father's medical treatment. He subsequently studied sociology and psychology at the University of Maryland, bringing contemporary social-science perspectives into his approach to community and education.

04 · 1997 · NORTHERN VIRGINIA

A community grows

In 1997, he joined the All Dulles Area Muslim Society (ADAMS) Center in Northern Virginia. His longstanding work there has included religious education, mentorship and community services. ADAMS currently identifies him as its Resident Scholar.

ADAMS Center profile ↗︎
05 · NATIONAL LEADERSHIP

Service beyond one community

His national service includes years of involvement with the Islamic Society of North America (ISNA), where he served in regional and executive roles and became president in 2010. His work has addressed education, family wellbeing, community leadership and dialogue across difference.

06 · INTERFAITH & INTERNATIONAL WORK

Building bridges across borders

His public work also extends to international interfaith cooperation and peacebuilding. Institutional biographies identify leadership roles with Religions for Peace and International Interfaith Peace Corps. The full archive will document the dates, projects and outcomes of those roles with primary sources.
